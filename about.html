About Us

Home-style cooking, made with care and served fresh.

Our story

Food that feels like home

Sri Lakshmi Home Foods is a home-based tiffin service dedicated to bringing fresh, hygienic, and delicious homemade meals to your doorstep. We believe that food made with care and quality ingredients can make a difference in everyday life.

Our meals are prepared using traditional recipes, maintaining authentic taste while ensuring modern hygiene standards. Whether you are a student, working professional, or someone who misses home-cooked food, we are here to serve you.

Our Mission

To provide healthy, affordable, and home-style meals that bring comfort and satisfaction to every customer.
